Collect Training Data

📸 What this does: Uses your webcam to collect face images for training a user-specific AI model.
When to use: BEFORE training - collect photos of faces to teach the model.
Result: Saves images to data/{user_id}/positive/ and data/{user_id}/negative/ folders.
Workflow: Collect Data → Train Model → Enroll/Recognize

Use your webcam to collect positive (user's images) and negative (other people's images) for training the Siamese network.

Instructions:

  1. Enter User ID: Enter the user ID (e.g., user_123) to collect data for
  2. Positive: Take multiple photos of the USER (different angles, expressions, lighting)
  3. Negative: Take photos of DIFFERENT people (not the user)
  4. Collect at least 20-50 positive images and 10-20 negative images for good results
  5. Images are saved to data/{user_id}/positive/ and data/{user_id}/negative/
  6. After collecting data, go to "Train Model" tab to start training for this user
